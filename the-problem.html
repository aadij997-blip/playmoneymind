The Problem

Students are leaving school without the money skills they need.

Financial literacy shapes confidence, choices, and opportunity. Yet too many young people reach adulthood without the basics.

Scroll to explore

Why it starts early

The gap starts before adulthood, and it compounds.

Kids face real financial decisions long before they reach adulthood, but many do not have access to the tools or knowledge to navigate them.

Without early exposure, small gaps in money skills can grow over time, making it harder to build confidence and financial stability later in life.

Money skills are easier to build before the stakes are high.

The cost of waiting

The cost of waiting is measured in missed practice.

Less confidence

Without early practice, money feels confusing and overwhelming, so students doubt their decisions.

Fewer choices

Delayed financial knowledge leads to limited options in college, career, and life.

More stress

Financial uncertainty creates ongoing anxiety that can affect mental health and well-being.

The solution has to change how students learn.
