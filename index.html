Our Story

MoneyMind Games didn't start in a boardroom. It started in a high school business class, with one question: what if learning personal finance could be as fun as playing a game?

That question became InvestQuest. It teaches kids to invest, budget, and handle taxes by playing, so the next generation can grow up with a stronger financial foundation.
